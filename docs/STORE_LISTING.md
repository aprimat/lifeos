## Description

LifeOS helps you turn meaningful goals into small, repeatable actions — and keep going when life gets busy.

**Set a direction.** Name a goal and, if you like, write down why it matters to you.

**Make a commitment.** Choose 30, 60 or 90 days (or ongoing), pick a few habits, and plan your rest days and travel in advance. Planned rest never counts as missed.

**Build or reduce.** Grow helpful habits with a minimum version for low-energy days, or step down unwanted ones — like scrolling — at your own pace.

**Follow the day.** Today shows only what's planned. One tap logs a session; the minimum version, a partial effort or a reschedule are always an option.

**See honest progress.** Consistency is measured against what you actually planned, with full and minimum sessions shown separately. There are no streaks to break and no life score.

**Reflect and adjust.** A short weekly review shows what worked and offers simple suggestions you can accept, edit or ignore. Nothing changes without you.

**Private by design.** Your data stays on your phone. LifeOS doesn't track you, and you can export or delete everything at any time.

LifeOS is not medical advice or addiction treatment. If you're reducing alcohol, smoking or other substances, please talk to a doctor or pharmacist.
